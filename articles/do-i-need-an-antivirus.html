Do I need an antivirus?

Many people install antivirus software immediately after buying a new computer, but with newer operating systems, is this still necesary?

What does an antivirus actually do?

An antivirus is a program that scans for computer for viruses and reports and removes them, like a doctor for your computer. Most modern antiviruses also include other features such as device scans, driver checks, and other status scans.

Microsoft Defender

In recent years, Microsoft defender has improved tons, and is actually a very useful tool. It offers virus & threat protection, account protection, firewall & network protection, app and browser control, device security, and device proformance & health reports.

Free vs. Paid

There are many paid options for antivirus softwares, but they may no longer be necesary. Microsoft defender offers many of the same tools and benefits as paid antivirus such as Norton and McAfee, with much less annoying notifications and ads asking you to pay more.
