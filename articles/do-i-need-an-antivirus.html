Do I need an antivirus?

Many people install antivirus software immediately after buying a new computer, but with newer operating systems, is this still necessary?

What does an antivirus actually do?

An antivirus is a program that scans your computer for viruses, reports and removes them, like a doctor for your computer. Most modern antiviruses also include features such as device scans, driver checks, and other status scans.

Microsoft Defender

In recent years, Microsoft Defender has improved significantly and is now a very useful tool. It offers virus & threat protection, account protection, firewall & network protection, app and browser control, device security, and device performance & health reports.

Free vs. Paid

There are many paid options for antivirus software, but they may no longer be necessary. Microsoft Defender offers many of the same tools and benefits as paid antivirus such as Norton and McAfee, with much less annoying notifications and ads asking you to pay more.

Safe Browsing

Browsing safely can help prevent attacks from happening in the first place. Be careful when opening attachments from emails, clicking on links, and visiting sketchy sites. Google has a great game about staying safe from email and text attacks, linked below and on the resources page.

Summary

In summary, with the improvements in built-in antivirus, purchased antiviruses are generally no longer necessary, and many users may not benefit from paying for additional features they already have built in.
